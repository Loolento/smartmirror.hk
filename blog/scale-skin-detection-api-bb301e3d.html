Oolaa Smart Mirror: Scale + Skin Detection API

Oct 5, 2026

Plenty of smart mirrors can show the time and the weather, play your music, or walk you through a workout — and most of them do more or less the same things. Oolaa's smart mirror refuses to stop there. We set out to turn the mirror from a screen that displays information into the health data center of your home.

Think about where health data usually ends up: scattered across apps you gradually stop opening. The scale has one app, the skincare gadget another, and none of them talk to each other. The Oolaa approach puts the data where you already stand every morning — at the mirror — and connects the devices directly to it.

An exclusive first: a scale that talks straight to the mirror

Weight management is the most basic form of health management, but the traditional routine is a hassle: step on the scale, then record the number yourself, or depend on a phone app to sync it. The Oolaa smart mirror exclusively supports a direct scale connection — the scale links to the mirror through an API.

Step on the scale, and your weight is transmitted to the mirror instantly, stored automatically, and ready to read back whenever you want it. There is no phone to open and nothing to type in. Your day-to-day weight changes are visible at a glance — a genuinely seamless smart living experience.

Skin detection, in the same mirror

Weight is only part of the picture. How your skin is doing is the other half of modern self-care, and it matters just as much to the people standing in front of the mirror each morning. The Oolaa smart mirror also supports a skin detection device, which measures the condition of your skin and sends the data to the mirror by API, to be stored alongside everything else.

Moisture, oil, elasticity — skin data like this only becomes meaningful when it is tracked over the long term. That is the only way to see whether a skincare product is actually working, or how your daily habits are affecting your skin. It is a level of health tracking that ordinary smart mirrors have never ventured into.

API integration: all your data, one platform

The crucial point is not any single feature. It is the integration. The scale and the skin analyzer both connect to the Oolaa smart mirror through APIs, and every reading can be browsed together, in real time, right at the mirror — no app to launch, just look.

Weight, body-fat trends, skin condition: health data from different dimensions, cross-referenced against each other. That is what a high-tech experience in the AI era should feel like, and that depth of API ecosystem integration is exactly what sets Oolaa apart.
