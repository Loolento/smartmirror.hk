Oolaa at Waldorf Astoria Bangkok

When a Waldorf Astoria selects a technology partner, the bar is not "does it work." The bar is: will it still feel luxurious in five years?

In March 2026, Oolaa completed deployment of smart mirror TVs across 140 guest rooms at the Waldorf Astoria Bangkok — a milestone that validates Oolaa's factory-direct approach in the most demanding hospitality segment.

5-star hotels don't buy products. They buy relationships that survive the 3am service call. Oolaa proved both.

The Brief

Waldorf Astoria Bangkok required bathroom mirror TVs that met three conditions simultaneously:

  • Invisible when off — a flawless mirror surface with zero bezel visibility
  • Instant-on — no boot sequence, no Android loading screen, no guest-facing technology
  • Silent operation — no fan noise in a bathroom environment designed for acoustic tranquillity

Conventional set-top box solutions failed on all three counts. Oolaa's embedded design — where processing, panel control, and connectivity live inside the mirror frame — met each requirement at the hardware level.

Custom Engineering

For this project, Oolaa engineered custom-sized mirror glass with edge-to-edge silvering — no bezel, no visible electronics. The embedded module was reworked for passive-only cooling, eliminating the need for any fan or vent. Power delivery runs through a single low-profile cable, concealed within the wall.

The software layer was customised to match Waldorf Astoria's brand guidelines: a dark, minimal UI that fades to mirror after 60 seconds of inactivity. Welcome screens display the guest's name on check-in day — pulled from the hotel PMS via Oolaa's API integration.

Installation

140 rooms. 14 days. One team. Oolaa's Hong Kong installation crew — licensed electricians with WR1 sign-off authority — flew to Bangkok and completed the deployment ahead of schedule. Pre-fabricated mounting brackets, pre-terminated cables, and a factory-run burn-in test on every unit meant zero on-site failures.

The hotel remained operational throughout. Oolaa's phased installation approach meant no more than two floors were offline at any moment.

What This Means

Waldorf Astoria Bangkok is not Oolaa's first international deployment — but it is the most prestigious. It demonstrates that factory-direct, Hong Kong-engineered smart mirrors can compete with — and outperform — the legacy AV brands that have dominated hotel technology for decades.

For project teams evaluating smart mirror suppliers: the Waldorf standard is achievable without the Waldorf markup. Oolaa's Shenzhen factory ships the same engineering to every project, regardless of scale.
