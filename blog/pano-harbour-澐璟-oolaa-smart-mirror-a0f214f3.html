Pano Harbour: Oolaa Smart Mirrors in Bathrooms

March 17, 2023

Welcome to Pano Harbour, the luxurious residential development where the bathroom cabinet turns out to be a screen. Oolaa Smart Mirrors are integrated into the bathroom cabinets at Pano Harbour, bringing the latest smart mirror technology — including voice control — into the daily routine of one of Hong Kong's luxury addresses. Tour the facilities, and the future of home living is already installed.

Built into the bathroom cabinets

The key word at Pano Harbour is integrated. The smart mirror is not an appliance hung on the wall after the fact; it is built into the bathroom cabinet itself, so the storage, the mirror, and the display arrive as one finished piece of furniture. Open the morning routine and everything is in its place: the mirror you groom in front of is the same surface that shows you the day ahead, and when the screen is off, the cabinet simply looks like a cabinet — clean lines, no visible electronics, nothing to date the room.

Integration also means nothing competes for the counter. Toiletries stay inside the cabinet, the mirror face stays clear, and the display never becomes one more object to move, charge, or clean around. In a luxury bathroom, that restraint is the point: the technology is present in the routine and absent from the room.

Voice control in the bathroom

The mirrors at Pano Harbour carry advanced functionality such as voice control. In a bathroom, voice is not a novelty; it is the right interface. Hands are wet, busy, or holding a toothbrush, and nobody wants to tap through menus with soapy fingers. A spoken request — for information, for music, for the day's arrangements — keeps the routine moving. Oolaa's smart mirrors support voice control including Cantonese through Google Assistant, so residents can speak naturally, in their mother tongue.

Specifying mirrors for a luxury development

For developers, cabinet integration raises the bar in a useful way. The mirror must be sized and finished with the cabinetry, installed as part of the fit-out rather than after it, and consistent across every bathroom in the project. That favors a manufacturer with its own factory and a long installation record. Oolaa (Viewdex Limited) is a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen and more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau; at Pano Harbour, that experience is built into the cabinets. More about the development is on the Pano Harbour website.
