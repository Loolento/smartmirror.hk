Pano Harbour: Oolaa Smart Mirrors in Bathrooms

March 17, 2023

Welcome to Pano Harbour, a luxurious residential development in Hong Kong — and to a bathroom experience that points at the future. Tour the facilities and you will find Oolaa Smart Mirror integrated directly into the bathroom cabinets: cutting-edge smart mirrors with the latest technology, including advanced voice-control functionality. It is luxury you can talk to.

Luxury Developments Are Competing on the Bathroom

In Hong Kong’s prime residential market, developers differentiate less on square footage — which everyone can measure — and more on the experience of living there, which buyers feel. The bathroom has become one of the decisive battlegrounds: it is intimate, it is used intensely every day, and it is where a development’s quality is hardest to fake. Stone, brassware, and lighting all speak — and now, so does the mirror.

Buyers at this level have stayed in the world’s best hotels. They recognise a standard mirror instantly, and they notice when something is better. A smart mirror integrated into the cabinetry signals that the developer thought about the bathroom as a living space rather than a wet room with fittings — and that signal lands exactly where purchase decisions are made: in the feeling of the place.

Integrated into the Cabinetry: Technology That Disappears

The defining detail at Pano Harbour is integration. The smart mirror is not mounted onto the bathroom — it is part of the bathroom cabinet itself, sitting flush within the joinery as if the cabinet was designed around it. Which, of course, it was: cabinet-integrated installation is decided at the design stage, with power, dimensions, and fixing points coordinated between the mirror supplier and the cabinet maker.

This is the luxury approach to bathroom technology, and it is worth understanding why it matters. A surface-mounted screen, however good, always reads as an addition. An integrated mirror reads as architecture. The display appears within the mirror surface only when needed — showing time, weather, news, or whatever the household wants at a glance — and vanishes back into a perfect mirror when not. The bathroom keeps its calm, uncluttered lines; the technology is there exactly when you want it and invisible when you don’t.

For developers, the lesson is procedural: involve the smart-mirror supplier while the bathroom is still a drawing. Retrofitting integration after the cabinets are built is somewhere between difficult and impossible; planning it from the start is straightforward. Oolaa’s project teams are built for exactly this coordination, with 20+ years of manufacturing and an own factory in Shenzhen behind them.

Voice Control in the Bathroom: Made for Real Mornings

The Pano Harbour mirrors feature advanced voice-control functionality — and the bathroom turns out to be one of the best rooms for it. Your hands are wet, your eyes are on the mirror, and you want information without touching anything: the weather before you dress, the traffic before you leave, the headlines while you shave. Voice is the interface that fits the moment.

With Google Assistant and Cantonese voice support, the mirror understands natural speech — no need to dry your hands to tap a screen. Ask for the day’s forecast, play the morning news, or set a reminder, all while getting ready. Combined with motion detection that wakes the display as you enter, the experience feels less like operating a device and more like the room anticipating you.

And the fundamentals are covered for the wet environment: IP65 sealing against steam and splashes — above alternatives like ORVIBO’s IPX4 — plus a built-in defogger so the display stays clear. A luxury bathroom mirror must first be a flawless mirror; everything else is built on top of that promise.

Frequently asked questions

What does “integrated into the cabinetry” mean?

It means the smart mirror is built into the bathroom cabinet itself — flush with the joinery, with power and fixings coordinated during manufacture — rather than mounted on the finished wall afterwards. The result looks like architecture, not an appliance.

Can I talk to the bathroom mirror?

Yes. With voice control via Google Assistant, including Cantonese support, you can ask for weather, news, and traffic, set reminders, and more — all without touching the mirror with wet hands.

Is a smart mirror safe in a steamy bathroom?

Oolaa bathroom mirrors are IP65 sealed against steam and water jets — a higher rating than alternatives such as ORVIBO IPX4 — and include a built-in defogger so the display stays clear.

Can an existing bathroom be upgraded to an integrated mirror?

True cabinet integration is easiest in a new or renovated bathroom, where the cabinet can be built around the mirror. For existing bathrooms, a surface-mounted smart mirror that replaces the current mirror is the practical upgrade path.
