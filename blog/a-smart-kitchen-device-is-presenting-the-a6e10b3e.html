Oolaa Smart Kitchen Display for Happy Cooking

Aug 12, 2022

Software decides whether a kitchen screen gets used or gets ignored. Oolaa's in-house software engineering team set out to build the friendliest possible interface for our Smart Kitchen Display — an ultimate user-friendly interface whose whole job is to help the one you love have happy cooking in the kitchen, every day.

An interface built in-house, for the kitchen

The interface was not adapted from a living-room product or licensed from a generic platform. Oolaa's own software engineers developed it, which means every screen, every button, and every layer of the experience was designed by people who answer for the product. That control matters in a kitchen, where the user is mid-recipe, hands busy, attention divided — and has no patience for a menu that makes her stop and think.

A kitchen interface succeeds when it disappears. The right information at the right size, reachable in the fewest possible steps, readable at a glance from the stove: that is what "user-friendly" has to mean where the cooking happens.

Building the interface in-house changes what happens after installation, too. When the people who designed the screens are the people who make the product, feedback from real kitchens travels a short road — from a user's comment to the team's next revision. There is no waiting on a platform vendor's roadmap and no negotiating with someone else's priorities. The interface belongs to the product, and the product team answers for it.

Happy cooking, every day

The goal is in the brief Oolaa gave itself: happy cooking, every day. Not a novelty for the first week, not a demo for guests — a display that earns its place in the daily rhythm of the kitchen, helping the cook the way a good tool does. Quietly, reliably, and without ever becoming one more thing to manage.

Kitchens are where Oolaa began: we are Hong Kong's market leader in kitchen TV, and the Smart Kitchen Display carries that heritage into the smart era — a screen designed for the room where families actually gather.

Picture the working kitchen it serves: a recipe followed step by step without a cookbook propped open, a timer kept in view while both hands are in the dough, the day's information caught up on while the kettle boils. None of that needs a complicated system. It needs a display that respects the cook's attention — present when wanted, quiet when not, and always exactly where the work is happening.

Sized for your cabinets

Kitchens are not built to one template, and neither is the display. The Smart Kitchen Display is made to fit the kitchen cabinet it lives with — and if you are looking for a bigger size for your kitchen cabinet, that conversation is exactly what our team is for. Talk to us about the kitchen you are building, and we will match the display to it.

Behind the product stands Oolaa (Viewdex Limited) — a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen and more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau. Factory-direct, in-house software, and an interface designed for one thing: the cook you love, cooking happy.

Fitting a display into a kitchen is a design exercise before it is a technology one. The screen has to sit at a height the cook can read, in a finish the kitchen can carry, at a size the cabinets can hold — which is why the sizing conversation matters as much as the software. Get those three right, and the interface the team built gets the setting it was designed for: close, clear, and welcome every single day.
