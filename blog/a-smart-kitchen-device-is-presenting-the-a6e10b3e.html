Oolaa Smart Kitchen Display for Happy Cooking

Aug 12, 2022

The kitchen is the most demanding room in the house for any screen — steam, splashes, wet hands, and a cook whose eyes belong on the pot, not the display. Oolaa's in-house software engineering answered with a purpose-built interface for the smart kitchen device: big, calm, and glanceable, designed to help the people you love cook happily every day. And for larger kitchens, bigger sizes are available to fit the kitchen cabinet — just ask.

Designed around the cook, not the spec sheet

Most screens are designed for clean hands and full attention. A kitchen screen gets neither. Oolaa's interface starts from that reality: touch targets large enough for a knuckle or an elbow, text with the contrast to survive across-the-room viewing, and layouts that can be read in a two-second glance between stirring and seasoning.

This is what “ultimate user-friendly” actually means in a kitchen. It is not more features; it is fewer demands. The interface does not ask the cook to wash, dry, unlock, and aim — it meets the cook mid-recipe, mid-mess, mid-thought, and still works. That restraint is only possible because the software is written in-house: every screen was drawn for the kitchen, not inherited from a phone.

What it shows: the kitchen's greatest hits

A recipe video, playing at eye level while both hands stay in the dough. A timer you can read from the stove without leaning in. A video call with family propped nowhere, because the display is already mounted where the action is. The morning news over coffee. A child's homework video mirrored from a phone while dinner comes together — “No Login. Just Cast.” means the phone never leaves a pocket.

None of these needs a tech enthusiast to appreciate. They need a screen that behaves like kitchen equipment: always ready, unbothered by the environment, and obvious to anyone who walks in. That is the standard Oolaa's interface was built to.

Sized for real kitchens — including yours

Kitchens are not standard, so the display should not be either. Oolaa's smart kitchen devices come in sizes to suit compact galleys and generous family kitchens alike — and if you are looking for a bigger size for your kitchen cabinet, it is available. A larger display turns the cabinet run into a genuine media wall: recipes at a readable distance, video the whole family can see, and a presence that anchors an open-plan kitchen.

Sizing advice is part of the service, not an afterthought. Viewing distance, cabinet depth, splash exposure, and sightlines from the dining area all shape the right choice — which is why Oolaa invites a conversation rather than a checkout click. Reach out on WhatsApp and describe the kitchen; the recommendation follows the room.
