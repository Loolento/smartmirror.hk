Oolaa Ai-Play Mirror Debuts at HSITP Campus

March 22, 2025

The Oolaa Ai-Play Mirror has unveiled its first project. The interactive gaming mirror is now at Buildings 8 and 9 of the Hong Kong-Shenzhen Innovation and Technology Park (HSITP), bringing a new kind of active entertainment to the park's community.

Fitness and play, in one mirror

The initiative behind the installation is simple: combine fitness and play. Instead of treating exercise as a separate chore scheduled for later, the Ai-Play Mirror folds movement into games — people play, and the fitness happens along the way. It is a fresh approach to health, and it is offered to all members of the community, not only the already-sporty. That matters: the hardest part of staying active is starting, and play lowers the starting line for everyone.

Why a first project matters

Every product line has to prove itself somewhere real. A campus setting like HSITP's Buildings 8 and 9 puts the mirror in front of a living, working community — people moving through shared spaces every day, which is exactly the environment an interactive mirror is made for. A first project is where a new idea stops being a concept and becomes part of daily life, used by real people in a real place.

Part of a bigger picture

The Ai-Play Mirror is one of Oolaa's eight product lines, developed by Hong Kong company Oolaa (Viewdex Limited) — factory-direct from its own factory in Shenzhen, with more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau.
