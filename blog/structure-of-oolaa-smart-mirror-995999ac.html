Inside an Oolaa Smart Mirror: 3-Part Structure

April 15, 2024

Every Oolaa smart mirror is divided into three parts. Part A is the mirror — or glass — surface, with its touch film. Part B is the LED panel. Part C is the motherboard, with the metal back cover of the whole smart mirror. Three parts, each with a clear job; watch the video above for the walkthrough, and read on for what each part actually does.

Part A: Mirror surface + touch film

The mirror surface is what you see, and what you use, every day — a true mirror first, which is why the unit still looks and behaves like a mirror in the room. Bonded with it is the touch film: the layer that registers your taps and swipes through the glass. That is how a mirror with no buttons and no exposed controls can still be fully touch-operated — the sensing happens at the surface itself.

Part B: LED panel

Behind the mirror surface sits the LED panel — the display of the smart mirror. When content plays, the panel's light comes through the mirror and your video, information or interface appears on the glass. When the panel is off, the mirror is simply a mirror again. The panel is the part that turns a wall fixture into a screen, without the room having to give up the mirror to get one.

Part C: Motherboard + metal back cover

The brains of the mirror are its motherboard — the board that runs the system and connects the parts together. It is covered by a metal back cover that spans the whole smart mirror, protecting the electronics inside and giving the unit its finished, rigid back. Surface, display, brain: that is the whole anatomy.

Why the structure matters

A clear three-part structure is more than tidy engineering. For buyers and project teams, it makes the mirror easier to specify, install and service with confidence: the surface you interact with, the panel you watch, and the electronics that drive it are distinct, purposeful layers. When you compare smart mirrors, ask how they are put together — the answer tells you a lot about how the product will live in a wall for years to come.

Key facts

  • Part A: mirror (glass) surface plus touch film — the reflection you see, and the touch layer you control through the glass.
  • Part B: LED panel — the display that shows content through the mirror, and disappears when off.
  • Part C: motherboard, protected by a metal back cover spanning the whole smart mirror.
  • The three-part construction is the standard anatomy of an Oolaa smart mirror.
