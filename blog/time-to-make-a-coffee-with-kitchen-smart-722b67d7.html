Make Coffee with a Kitchen Smart Mirror by Oolaa

August 12, 2022

It's time to have a nice coffee with the Kitchen Smart Mirror. Watch the moment in the video above: the coffee is brewing, and the cabinet door in front of you is not just a cabinet door. That is the whole idea of the product — technology that joins the best minutes of the kitchen day instead of interrupting them.

A cabinet door that's also a computer

The Kitchen Smart Mirror works as an interactive kitchen cabinet door. Open the cabinet, and it is a door. Close it, and it presents the features of a computer — information and interaction, right where the cook is standing. No extra device on the counter, no tablet propped against the backsplash, no phone balanced by the sink. The computing lives in a surface the kitchen already has.

That placement is the product's intelligence. Kitchens run out of counter space long before they run out of needs, and a cabinet door is prime territory: at eye level, in the middle of the action, and otherwise doing nothing at all.

Eye level is the other half of the design. A counter display angles up at you; a phone leans where it can. A cabinet door is simply there, at the height of your face, in the part of the kitchen where you already stand to work. Information placed there costs no effort to use — a glance is the whole interaction — and in a room where hands are wet, full, or busy, a glance is often all anyone has to spare.

Hidden behind a stylish surface

When it is not in use, the technology disappears. The Kitchen Smart Mirror presents a stylish surface that belongs to the kitchen's design — a mirror finish, not a gadget's glow. Kitchens are designed rooms, often the most designed room in the home, and a screen that shouts "device" fights the cabinetry around it. This one doesn't. It waits, handsomely, until it is wanted.

That disappearing act is harder than it looks, and it is where Oolaa's years of mirror engineering show. A surface has to be a true mirror, a working display, and a cabinet door, all in the same few millimeters — and it has to keep being all three, every day, in the steam and splashes of a working kitchen.

The coffee ritual is the honest test of any kitchen technology, because it happens on autopilot. Nobody wants to learn a system before the first cup. The Kitchen Smart Mirror passes that test by asking for nothing: it is a door when you reach for the cups, a mirror while the kitchen wakes up, and a computer only in the moments you actually want one.

Made for kitchen moments

Think of the moments the kitchen actually runs on: the first coffee of the morning, the quiet minute while something simmers, the recipe checked one more time. The Kitchen Smart Mirror was made for those moments — the information of the day on the glass while the coffee brews, help at eye level while dinner comes together, and a beautiful surface the rest of the time.
