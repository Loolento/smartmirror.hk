Make Coffee with a Kitchen Smart Mirror by Oolaa

August 12, 2022

It’s time to make a nice coffee — with a little help from the Kitchen Smart Mirror. As an interactive kitchen cabinet door, Oolaa’s kitchen screen convinces with the features of a full computer, hidden behind a stylish surface. Recipes, news, music, and video calls: everything you want in the kitchen, on the surface that was already there.

The Kitchen Cabinet Door That Is Secretly a Computer

The concept is disarmingly simple: take the kitchen cabinet door or the backsplash surface you already have, and put a computer behind it. When the display is off, it is a normal, stylish kitchen surface — a mirror-finish door that reflects the room. Wake it, and it becomes a full interactive screen: touch, voice, apps, streaming, and smart-home control.

“The features of a computer” is not marketing shorthand. The Kitchen Smart Mirror runs the applications a kitchen actually needs: video for recipes and entertainment, a browser for looking things up mid-cook, music and podcast apps, and smart-home dashboards for the rest of the house. Because it is built for the kitchen, the surface is sealed and easy to wipe clean — a quick pass with a cloth deals with steam and splashes.

The elegance is in what it doesn’t do: it doesn’t take up counter space, it doesn’t add another gadget to charge, and it doesn’t clutter the one room where clutter matters most. In Hong Kong kitchens, where every centimetre is contested, a screen that lives inside the cabinetry is the only screen that makes sense.

Morning Rituals, Upgraded: Coffee, News, and the Day Ahead

Consider the most ordinary — and therefore most important — use case: the morning coffee. You pad into the kitchen, start the machine, and while it brews, the cabinet door shows you the overnight headlines, today’s weather, and the traffic on your route to work. Two minutes, zero friction, and you leave the house informed.

Then there is the evening version: dinner prep with a recipe video playing at eye level, pausable by voice when your hands are covered in flour. Or the weekend version: a long, slow cook with a podcast or a football match keeping you company. Or the social version: friends gathered around the island, the kitchen screen showing photos from the last trip while everyone cooks together.

None of these scenarios requires learning a new system. If you can use a phone, you can use the kitchen mirror — and with “No Login. Just Cast.”, the family can throw content from their own devices onto the big kitchen screen without accounts or passwords. The technology bends to the household’s habits, not the other way around.

Why the Kitchen Is the Next Screen in the Home

Step back and the pattern is clear: every room in the home has gained its screen — the living room long ago, the bedroom more recently, the bathroom with the mirror TV. The kitchen was last precisely because it is the hardest room: heat, steam, grease, wet hands, and no spare surfaces. Solving the kitchen screen meant solving all of that at once, which is why the cabinet-door form factor matters — it answers the space, durability, and cleanliness questions in a single design decision.

There is also a social shift behind it. The kitchen has become the most-used room in many Hong Kong homes: working from home turned it into a daytime office annex, and compact flats turned it into the natural gathering point. A screen that belongs there — showing recipes while you cook, news while you eat breakfast at the counter, video calls with family while you prep — simply matches how the room is actually lived in.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is a Kitchen Smart Mirror?

It is a kitchen cabinet door (or wall surface) with a computer and display built in. Off, it looks and works like a normal stylish surface; on, it is a full interactive touchscreen for recipes, entertainment, and smart-home control.

Will steam and cooking grease damage it?

It is designed for the kitchen environment: a sealed, wipe-clean surface that handles steam and splashes, unlike an ordinary tablet or TV. A quick wipe with a cloth is the whole cleaning routine.

Does it take up counter or wall space?

No — that is the point. It lives inside the cabinetry you already have, so it adds a large screen to the kitchen without occupying a single centimetre of counter or new wall space.

Can I control it without touching it?

Yes. Alongside touch, it supports Cantonese voice control — perfect for setting timers, pausing recipe videos, or playing music when your hands are busy, wet, or covered in flour.
