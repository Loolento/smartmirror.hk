Oolaa Ai-Play Mirror: AR Interactive Gaming Mirror

Aug 11, 2025

The Oolaa Ai-Play Mirror is Oolaa's interactive gaming mirror — a Hong Kong innovation built around a simple idea: a mirror can be a place to play, move and unwind, not just a place to check your reflection. This post is part 2 of our six-part series on the Ai-Play Mirror, and it is all about one game: the Kung Fung Game.

The Kung Fung Game

Kung fu is movement, rhythm and timing — which makes it a natural fit for an interactive mirror. In the Kung Fung Game, the play happens right in front of the mirror: you are in the game and in the reflection at the same time, moving with the action instead of sitting still with a controller in your hands. It is gaming that gets you off the couch by design, and it turns a spare few minutes into something active, social and genuinely fun.

A relief mirror, not just another screen

The Ai-Play Mirror was conceived as a relief mirror: its job is to help people shake off stress. Interactive play does that differently from passive entertainment. You move, you react, you laugh at yourself in the mirror — and a short session of play can reset a long day in a way that scrolling a phone never will. Relief, here, is not a side effect of the product; it is the point of it.

Where an interactive gaming mirror fits

Interactive mirrors make sense wherever people gather, wait or work out: a family living room, a home gym corner, a clubhouse games area, a school or wellness space. The mirror format matters when you are choosing: when the game is off, it is still a mirror, so it needs no dedicated floor space and never looks like arcade equipment parked in the room. Look at the space in front of the mirror first — players need room to move safely, with the play area kept clear.
