Voice assistants are nothing new. But a voice assistant that truly understands Cantonese is still a rare thing. The Oolaa smart mirror has Cantonese voice built in, letting you talk to your mirror in the language you actually speak at home — and turning it into a genuine private assistant.

Watch the demo above: this is the Oolaa smart mirror running through Google Assistant, in Cantonese.

A smart mirror that speaks Cantonese

There is no need to switch languages, and no need to fall back on Mandarin or English to be understood. Ask the mirror a question in Cantonese and it answers right back, instantly. That zero-distance interaction — you speak naturally, and you are understood — is what high technology is supposed to feel like: the machine adapting to the person, not the other way around.

Your private assistant, one sentence away

The weather before you dress. Your schedule for the day. Your health data. Music for the routine. With the Oolaa smart mirror, all of it is handled with a single sentence in Cantonese — ask, and it is done, the way a private assistant would handle it.

Voice control proves its worth most when your hands are busy: in the middle of a skincare routine, halfway through your makeup, or fresh from a workout. Those are exactly the moments when reaching for a phone breaks the flow — and exactly when a spoken sentence beats a touchscreen. The mirror stops being a screen you operate and becomes an assistant you simply talk to.

Why Cantonese matters

Cantonese is the everyday language of Hong Kong — the language of kitchens, family dinners and daily life. A technology product that can only understand a foreign language delivers a permanently discounted experience: every interaction asks you to translate yourself first, and that small friction, repeated daily, quietly pushes features out of use. Oolaa insists on getting Cantonese voice right so that smart living can truly land — rooted in the way Hong Kong people actually live, every single day.

AI-driven: it understands context, and keeps learning

Hearing the words is only the beginning. Backed by AI, the Oolaa smart mirror goes beyond transcription to understand context — what you mean, not just what you said — and the more you use it, the smoother and more personal the experience becomes. That depth is something ordinary smart mirrors simply do not offer: not a display that takes commands, but an assistant that learns the shape of your day.
