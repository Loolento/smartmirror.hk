No Login. Just Cast.

Every hotel guest has experienced this: you check in, walk into the room, and want to watch your own Netflix. You grab the remote. You navigate to the app. You're prompted to log in. You type your email and password on a TV remote. Three minutes later, you give up.

Oolaa Mirror TV solves this by removing the login entirely.

Phone authenticates. Mirror plays. No usernames. No passwords. No QR-code gymnastics.

How It Works

When a guest enters the room, their phone is already on the hotel Wi-Fi. Oolaa Mirror TV detects the device via a secure local handshake — no cloud account, no stored credentials. The guest taps "Cast" on their phone, and content appears on the mirror. That's it.

Behind the scenes, Oolaa uses a custom protocol built on WebRTC and local-network discovery. The mirror runs a lightweight Oolaa Cast Receiver (a secure, read-only embedded module). The phone runs nothing — it uses the native OS casting framework. There is nothing to install, nothing to log into, and nothing that leaves the local network.

Why Hotels Care

For hotel operators, guest logins are a support headache. Forgotten passwords. Account lockouts. Check-out day panic when the next guest sees the previous guest's Netflix profile. Oolaa eliminates every one of these problems at the hardware level.

The mirror forgets everything on check-out. Zero guest data stored. GDPR-compliant by design.

What This Unlocks

Zero-login casting is the foundation for Oolaa's wider hospitality vision: personalised welcome screens, in-mirror room service ordering, spa booking, and local area guides — all authenticated by the phone you already have in your pocket, without a single password.
