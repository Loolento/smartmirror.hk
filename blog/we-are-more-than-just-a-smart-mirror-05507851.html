Oolaa: More Than a Smart Mirror Maker

August 12, 2022

Oolaa is best known for its signature smart mirror — but the mirror was never the whole story. Behind more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau stands a company that designs total smart home solutions. One demonstration says it all: a wireless charger buried beneath 20mm of solid granite, still detecting and charging with full strength. That is the kind of engineering you only get from a team that builds, not just sells.

A total-solution mindset, not a product catalogue

Most technology vendors sell you a box. Oolaa starts from the home itself. When the company assists customers in designing a total solution for a quality smart home, the conversation begins with how people actually live: where they charge their phone, how they move through the morning routine, which rooms should respond to voice and which should stay quiet. The smart mirror may be the signature product, but it is one instrument in an orchestra.

This mindset matters most in real projects. A developer fitting out a residential tower does not need eleven suppliers and eleven excuses — they need one partner who can think across the bathroom, the kitchen, the foyer, and the front door. Oolaa's eight product lines exist for exactly this reason: Smart Mirror, Bath and Mirror TV, Kitchen and Smart TV, Outdoor TV, Ai-Play Mirror, Axis, Smart Bath Panel, and Ai School Kiosk. Each line was developed because a real project asked for it.

The result is a company that developers and designers treat as a systems partner rather than a gadget supplier. The mirror gets the headlines; the total solution is what gets specified into projects.

Engineering that hides in plain sight

Consider the granite demonstration. A 20mm slab of stone is, by every instinct, the enemy of wireless charging. Stone is thick, dense, and unforgiving — most consumer chargers fail through a few millimetres of phone case, let alone two centimetres of rock. Oolaa placed its wireless charger under that granite and it still detected the device and charged with strong, reliable power.

Why does this matter? Because the best smart home technology is invisible. A luxury kitchen island should be a seamless expanse of stone, not a stone top with an ugly plastic charging puck screwed onto it. A bedside table should be furniture, not a gadget dock. When the charging coil can live beneath the surface and still perform, designers get their clean lines and users get their convenience. Nothing is compromised.

This is the same philosophy behind the smart mirror itself: technology that disappears into the room until you need it. A mirror that shows the time, the weather, and your music — then becomes a plain, elegant mirror again. A bathroom TV that is invisible until it turns on. Engineering that hides in plain sight is harder to build than engineering that shows off, and it is the harder path Oolaa has consistently chosen.

Factory-direct: why building beats reselling

Oolaa (Viewdex Limited) is a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen. That single fact explains a great deal about the company's range. A trading company can only sell what its suppliers make; a factory-direct company can build what its customers need. The granite-hidden wireless charger, the custom-sized mirrors for hotel projects, the bathroom TVs engineered for humid environments — these exist because the team controls the manufacturing.

Factory-direct also changes the economics. Without layers of middlemen, project pricing stays honest, and custom work stays feasible. When a developer needs a non-standard size or a designer needs a finish that matches a specific stone, the answer can be yes instead of a shrug. Over 20+ years in business, this capability has compounded into the company's market-leading position in Hong Kong bathroom TV and kitchen TV.

It also underpins reliability. With 31,000+ installations across Hong Kong and Macau, Oolaa supports what it sells — spare parts, servicing, and upgrades come from the same team that built the product. For a developer handing over hundreds of units, that continuity is worth more than any single feature.

What this means for your project

If you are a developer, architect, or interior designer evaluating smart home partners, the granite test is a useful mental model. Ask not just "what does the product do" but "what can the company build." Can they hide the technology in your stone, your joinery, your mirror? Can they design the bathroom, the kitchen, and the foyer as one system instead of three purchases?

Oolaa's platform answers the systems question as well. The product lines support the full range of smart home protocols — Matter, Zigbee, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and KNX — and connect with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, and Xiaomi through Matter. Voice control runs through Google Assistant, with Cantonese supported for Hong Kong households. The foyer mirror can serve as a whole-home control hub, tying the rooms together. So the "more than a smart mirror" story is not just about more products — it is about one coherent system.
