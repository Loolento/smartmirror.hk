Oolaa: More Than a Smart Mirror Maker

August 12, 2022

Besides Oolaa's signature smart mirror, we also assist customers in designing total solutions for quality smart homes. Here is a sample that shows the quality of Oolaa's products — and what "total solution" means when it leaves the brochure and enters a real home.

The 20mm granite test

We put 20mm of granite over a wireless charger. The result: thanks to the charger's strong sensing, the phone on top charged smoothly — straight through the stone, as if the granite were not there.

Pause on why that matters. The enemy of a beautifully finished interior is visible hardware: pucks, pads and cables scattered across surfaces that were designed to be clean. A charger that works through 20mm of stone can disappear entirely beneath a countertop. The surface stays stone, the function stays available, and the technology stops asking to be looked at. That is the standard a total solution should be judged by — not how impressive the device looks, but how completely it can vanish into the home while still working.

Total solutions, not single products

A quality smart home is not one clever device. It is the pieces working together: the mirror in the bathroom, the television in the kitchen, the charging point hidden in the counter — specified as one design, not accumulated as a pile of gadgets bought one at a time.

That is the work Oolaa does beyond the smart mirror. The company's range spans eight product lines — Smart Mirror, Bath/Mirror TV, Kitchen/Smart TV, Outdoor TV, Ai-Play Mirror, Axis, Smart Bath Panel and Ai School Kiosk — and its design work ties installations together into a single, coherent smart home for each customer.

For the customer, the difference shows up in the decisions they never have to make twice. When one team designs the whole solution, the mirror, the screens and the hidden details like through-stone charging are considered together — how they look side by side, how they are controlled, how they will be serviced — instead of each product arriving with its own assumptions. The home ends up feeling designed, because it was.

Our mission

This is our mission: to provide stable and quality services to customers, and to ensure that users who choose us are satisfied with Oolaa. It is a plain promise, and it scales from a single charger hidden under granite to more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau, delivered over more than 20 years in business by a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen.

We are more than just a smart mirror. The mirror is where most people meet us — the granite counter is where they find out how far the work goes.
