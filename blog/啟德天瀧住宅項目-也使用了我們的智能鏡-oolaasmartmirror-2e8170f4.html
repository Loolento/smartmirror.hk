Kai Tak Homes Add Oolaa Smart Mirrors to Bathrooms

April 15, 2023

In a high-tech smart luxury home, the bathroom mirror has a bigger job than it used to. At this residential project in Kai Tak, the Oolaa smart mirror is part of the master bathroom — and a key element of the home's smart system, bringing the convenience and delight of technology into everyday life.

A key element of the master bathroom

The Oolaa smart mirror pairs a high-definition mirror surface with an intelligent voice assistant. Standing in front of it, residents hold one of the home's control points: through voice commands, they can control their home devices, manage their schedule, and even play music and movies. The mirror is no longer just a mirror — it is an entrance to smart living, making daily life easier and more relaxed.

Beauty and health, built into the routine

The mirror also offers personalized beauty advice and health data analysis, so residents can enjoy private, tailored beauty care in the comfort of their own bathroom. Skincare guidance and a read on your health, delivered where the day begins — that is the kind of surprise and convenience a high-tech mirror brings to its owner, every single morning.

Finished to match a luxury home

The design pays attention to detail, working together with the project's other luxurious finishes and fittings to create an ultimate quality of life. If you are looking for a high-tech, luxurious place to live, this Kai Tak project is an ideal choice — and the Oolaa smart mirror becomes an indispensable companion within it, making life more convenient, more comfortable and more colorful.
