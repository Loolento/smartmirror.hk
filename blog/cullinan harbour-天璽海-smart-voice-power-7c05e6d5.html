Cullinan Harbour Gets Oolaa Voice Power Switch

November 11, 2025

We are proud to introduce our latest innovation — the Smart Voice Power Switch by Oolaa, introduced for Cullinan Harbour. The switch is specially designed to connect with and control the defroster behind the master bathroom mirror, putting a small but daily piece of bathroom comfort under voice control.

A switch designed for one job: the mirror defroster

Behind a modern bathroom mirror sits a defroster — the heater that keeps the glass clear after a hot shower. It is the kind of feature residents use every single day, yet it is usually governed by an ordinary wall switch: one more switch to find, reach for and remember, often with wet hands.

The Smart Voice Power Switch was designed for exactly this job. It connects to the defroster behind the master bathroom mirror and controls it by voice, so clearing the mirror becomes something you simply ask for while you are already standing in front of it. It is a focused product — not a general gadget looking for a purpose, but a switch built around one real routine.

A drop-in replacement for the traditional 86 wall switch

The switch is the perfect replacement for traditional 86 wall switches — the standard switch format found on bathroom walls. Because it takes the place of the existing switch, it eliminates the need for extra wiring or conduit installations: no new cable runs to chase into the wall, and no additional conduit to plan around the vanity.

For a project team, that simplicity matters. A smarter bathroom usually means more wiring coordination between trades; here, the upgrade path is the switch itself. The control becomes wireless and voice-operated, while the installation stays as straightforward as the switch it replaces.

Voice control, a simpler routine

The result is what the product promises: a smarter, simpler and more convenient lifestyle with voice power control. The master bathroom keeps its clean lines — no extra panels or remotes — and the defroster answers to the voice instead of a fingertip.

At Cullinan Harbour, that detail sits behind the master bathroom mirror, quietly doing its job every morning. It is a small component with an outsized effect on how finished the bathroom feels: the technology is present, but the wall stays clean.
