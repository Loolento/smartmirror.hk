Cullinan Harbour Gets Oolaa Voice Power Switch

November 11, 2025

We are proud to introduce our latest innovation — the Smart Voice Power Switch by Oolaa, installed at Cullinan Harbour. Specially designed to connect with and control the defroster behind the master bathroom mirror, this product is the perfect replacement for a standard power switch: the same job, done by voice, with the intelligence of a full smart home behind it.

The foggy mirror problem — and why the defroster matters

Every Hong Kong household knows the scene: a hot shower, a bathroom full of steam, and a mirror rendered completely useless by condensation. You wipe it with a towel, it fogs again in thirty seconds, and the morning routine proceeds half-blind. The engineering answer is the defroster — a heating element behind the mirror glass that keeps the surface just warm enough to prevent condensation from forming.

Oolaa's bathroom mirrors include a built-in defogger as standard, alongside an IP65 rating for water and dust protection and motion detection. But a defroster is only useful if it is actually switched on when needed — and that is where most installations fall down. A conventional setup buries the defroster's power switch somewhere inconvenient: behind the mirror, inside a cabinet, or on a wall plate nobody can find with wet hands. The technology exists; the control does not.

The Smart Voice Power Switch solves the control problem at its root. Instead of hunting for a physical switch, the resident simply speaks. The defroster warms the mirror before the shower starts, the glass stays clear throughout, and the morning routine never misses a beat. Small product, large quality-of-life difference.

How the Smart Voice Power Switch works

The concept is elegantly simple: take the humble power switch — the most numerous electrical device in any home — and give it a voice. The Smart Voice Power Switch wires in as a direct replacement for a standard switch, so installation follows the familiar pattern any electrician knows. But once installed, it joins the smart home: voice commands through Google Assistant, with Cantonese supported, plus control through the broader Oolaa platform.

At Cullinan Harbour, the switch's job is specific: powering and controlling the defroster behind the master bathroom mirror. A typical usage pattern looks like this — the resident says a command on the way to the bathroom, the defroster begins warming the glass, and by the time the shower is over the mirror is perfectly clear. No fumbling behind the mirror, no forgotten switches left on all day, no fogged glass.

Because the switch is part of Oolaa's smart home ecosystem — supporting Matter, Zigbee, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and KNX, and connecting with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, and Xiaomi — it can also participate in automation. A "good morning" scene could warm the mirror, raise the bathroom lighting, and start the news briefing in one command. The switch is a single device, but it inherits the intelligence of the whole system.

Why voice control belongs in the bathroom

The bathroom is the room where voice control makes the most sense and touch control makes the least. Hands are wet, soapy, or holding something; the user is often mid-routine and cannot spare attention for a screen; and the devices being controlled — lights, exhaust fans, defrosters, music — are all simple on/off or level commands that voice handles perfectly.

This is why Oolaa's bathroom strategy pairs every touchpoint with a voice alternative. The smart mirror shows information on the glass and takes voice commands. The defroster, via the Smart Voice Power Switch, takes voice commands. Nothing in the bathroom demands dry hands or full attention — the room adapts to the resident's state, not the other way around. It is the same "happy technology" philosophy that runs through the company's kitchen and foyer products: technology should remove friction from real routines.

There is a practical side too. Voice control in Cantonese matters in Hong Kong — a smart home that only understands English is a smart home that half the household cannot use. Oolaa's voice control through Google Assistant supports Cantonese, so the whole family, across generations, can operate the bathroom's technology naturally.
