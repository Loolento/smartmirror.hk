Oolaa Smart Mirror in Luxury Serviced Apartments

February 23, 2025

Discover the epitome of comfort and innovation: Oolaa Smart Mirror has been installed in a prestigious serviced apartment project in Central, Hong Kong, near the renowned Four Seasons Hotel. Each luxurious bathroom features our cutting-edge smart mirror with a 32-inch display, seamlessly integrating technology into the most private room of the residence. For guests who have seen everything, the bathroom mirror that greets them with the day’s news, weather, and information is a genuine surprise.

Central’s Serviced Apartment Race — and Why Bathrooms Win It

Central is the most competitive serviced-apartment market in Hong Kong. Corporate tenants and relocating executives choosing between residences on Hong Kong Island compare the same fundamentals — location, layout, service — and then decide on feeling. Increasingly, that feeling is delivered by the details: the bathroom that feels like a five-star hotel suite, the technology that works without a manual, the sense that the residence was designed for the way they actually live.

The bathroom punches far above its floor area in this contest. It is where the guest starts and ends every day, and it is where a residence can most clearly signal quality. A standard mirror says “functional.” A 32-inch smart mirror — large, bright, and alive with useful information — says “this place was thought through.” For operators competing for long-stay corporate contracts, that signal converts directly into occupancy and rate.

A 32-Inch Smart Mirror in Every Bathroom

Size matters in a bathroom, and 32 inches is a deliberate choice: large enough to read the news from across the room while you get ready, yet proportioned to sit elegantly above the vanity. Behind the genuine mirror surface, the display shows the time, weather, headlines, and traffic at a glance — the exact information a Central executive needs before heading to the office.

Integration is the quiet engineering behind the effect. The mirror replaces the standard bathroom mirror one-for-one, with IP65 sealing against steam and splashes, a built-in defogger so the display stays clear, and a finish that matches the bathroom’s design language rather than fighting it. Guests do not see “a screen bolted to the wall”; they see a beautiful mirror that happens to be intelligent. That distinction is the difference between technology that impresses on day one and technology that still delights on day one hundred.

“No Login. Just Cast.” — Hospitality Without the Helpdesk Calls

Here is the operational detail that serviced-apartment operators care about most: Oolaa’s flagship “No Login. Just Cast.” approach means guests can throw content from their own phones onto the bathroom mirror without accounts, passwords, or a call to reception. In hospitality, every avoided helpdesk call is money saved and a frustration prevented.

Think about the guest journey. A newly arrived executive, jet-lagged, wants to watch the news while showering — no login screens, no hotel Wi-Fi portal gymnastics, just cast and watch. A long-stay tenant follows a workout video in the bathroom mirror in the morning. When the guest checks out, there is nothing to log out of and no personal data left behind on the device, because nothing was ever logged into. It is the rare piece of in-room technology that is simultaneously more capable and less troublesome than what it replaces.

What Hotel and Serviced-Apartment Operators Should Specify

If you operate or develop serviced residences, three specification lessons emerge from projects like this Central installation. First, standardise the smart mirror across every bathroom category — consistency is what makes the technology feel like part of the brand rather than a gimmick in the penthouse. Second, choose a supplier with hospitality-grade reliability and local support: with 31,000+ installations across Hong Kong and Macau and 20+ years in business, Oolaa’s own factory in Shenzhen and Hong Kong-based teams mean parts and service are never far away.

Third, think about the information layer, not just the hardware. A smart mirror’s value compounds when it carries genuinely useful content — weather, traffic, building notices, local recommendations. Frequently asked questions

Why put a smart mirror in a serviced apartment bathroom?

The bathroom is where guests start and end each day, so it shapes their impression of the whole residence. A smart mirror delivers useful information — time, weather, news, traffic — at exactly the moment guests need it, and differentiates the property in Central’s competitive market.

Is a 32-inch display too large for a bathroom?

Not at typical viewing distances. Thirty-two inches is readable from across the room while remaining proportioned above a standard vanity. It is large enough for news and video, yet still functions perfectly as an everyday mirror when the display is off.

That is the point of “No Login. Just Cast.” — guests cast content from their own phones with no accounts, no passwords, and no instructions from reception. The ambient information (time, weather, news) is simply there, every morning.

Standardising one model across all bathrooms simplifies spares and servicing enormously. With a Hong Kong company, an own factory in Shenzhen, and local installation teams, support and replacement parts are close at hand — a key reason developers choose Oolaa for multi-unit projects.
