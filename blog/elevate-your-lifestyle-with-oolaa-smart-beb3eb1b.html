Oolaa Smart Mirror in Luxury Serviced Apartments

February 23, 2025

Discover the epitome of comfort and innovation with the Oolaa Smart Mirror, recently installed in a prestigious serviced apartment project in Central, Hong Kong, near the renowned Four Seasons Hotel. In residences where guests and residents expect a hotel standard of finish, the bathroom mirror has quietly become a screen, a control point and a piece of the interior design — all at once.

A 32-inch smart mirror in every bathroom

Each luxurious bathroom in the project features our cutting-edge smart mirror, with a 32-inch display. That size matters in a serviced apartment bathroom: large enough to read comfortably and to enjoy content, proportioned to sit naturally above the vanity. The display is there when wanted — information and entertainment during the morning routine — and the mirror remains, above all, an elegant bathroom mirror.

For serviced residences, consistency is part of the luxury. Every bathroom offering the same considered experience means every resident and guest gets the same standard, whether they stay for a month or a year.

Home automation with an elegant face

The mirror seamlessly integrates home automation with an elegant design. It provides a perfect blend of functionality and entertainment, giving users quick access to information while enhancing their daily routines. Instead of a wall of switches and separate devices, the resident meets one calm surface: the mirror is where the home's technology presents itself, and it does so without disturbing the bathroom's design language.

That is the promise behind the project — where technology meets lifestyle. The smart mirror does not ask residents to change how they live; it upgrades the moments they already have: getting ready, winding down, checking the day ahead.

Why serviced apartments specify smart mirrors

Serviced apartments compete on experience. A smart mirror supports that experience in practical ways: residents get quick access to information without calling the front desk, entertainment is built into the room rather than added as an afterthought, and the property presents a single, coherent standard of technology across every unit. For operators, a factory-direct supplier matters too — Oolaa (Viewdex Limited) is a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen and more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau, so specification, supply and support come from one accountable source.
