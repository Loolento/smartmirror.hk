Oolaa Mirror TV at Park Hyatt Niseko Ski Resort

Feb 18, 2025

For avid skiers, Park Hyatt Niseko offers a luxurious five-star escape like no other — and the luxury does not stop at the bathroom door. An Oolaa Mirror TV is installed in every bathroom at the resort, a smart addition designed to enhance each stay.

A smarter bathroom at a five-star ski resort

Hotel bathrooms are where a stay is quietly judged. The towels, the water pressure, the light — and increasingly, the technology. At Park Hyatt Niseko, the bathroom mirror doubles as a television, so a guest can catch the weather and the snow report while getting ready for the mountain, or sink into the bath in the evening with a favorite show playing where the mirror was a moment ago. It is the kind of detail guests photograph, mention in reviews, and remember when they choose where to stay next winter.

The coating that makes it possible

The Oolaa Mirror TV features a special-made mirror coating that makes watching TV on the mirror a reality. Getting that balance right is the whole craft: reflective enough to be a true mirror when the screen is off, clear enough to be a proper television when it is on. That coating transforms the bathroom into a high-tech oasis — a place to unwind with your favorite shows while relaxing after a day on the slopes, surrounded by the comfort the Park Hyatt name promises.

Built around guest happiness

At Park Hyatt, the priority is the guest's happiness and comfort, and the mirror TV serves both. After hours on the mountain, the bathroom becomes a private retreat: warm water, a familiar program, no phone balanced on the sink. The resort's commitment to luxury and innovation redefines what it means to stay in a high-end hotel — not with technology that shouts, but with technology placed exactly where it improves the stay.

It is also why hotels keep specifying mirror TVs. Oolaa has delivered more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau, from residences to five-star hotels, with the mirror TV manufactured in its own factory in Shenzhen. For a hotel, that means one supplier that designs, builds, and stands behind the screen in every bathroom.
