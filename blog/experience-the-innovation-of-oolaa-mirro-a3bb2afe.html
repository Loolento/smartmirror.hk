Oolaa Mirror TV at Park Hyatt Niseko Ski Resort

Feb 18, 2025

For avid skiers, Park Hyatt Niseko offers a luxurious five-star escape like no other. Indulge in the ultimate comfort with the Oolaa Mirror TV — a smart addition installed in every bathroom, designed to enhance your stay. The Oolaa Mirror TV features a special-made mirror coating that makes watching TV on a mirror possible: when the screen is on, it is a television; when it is off, it is simply a beautiful mirror.

A mirror by day, a television by night

The magic of a mirror TV lies in its coating. A conventional mirror reflects everything and shows nothing; a conventional television shows everything and reflects nothing. The Oolaa Mirror TV uses a special-made mirror coating engineered to do both: reflective enough to work as a true mirror when the display is off, yet transparent enough to let the picture shine through clearly when it is on. The result is a bathroom fixture that never compromises the interior design — there is no black rectangle on the wall, no visible technology until the moment you want it.

For a five-star property like Park Hyatt Niseko, that invisibility is the whole point. Luxury hotel bathrooms are composed down to the last detail, and a standard TV would break the composition. The mirror TV preserves the calm, material-led design of the bathroom while adding an entertainment layer that guests discover for themselves.

Why a ski resort bathroom is the perfect setting

Think about the rhythm of a ski holiday. You return from the slopes physically spent, and the next hour belongs to the bathroom: the long hot shower, the slow soak, the unhurried routine of warming up and winding down. It is the part of the day when you most want entertainment — and the part of the hotel room where entertainment has traditionally been absent.

A mirror TV in every bathroom meets that moment exactly. Guests can catch up on the news, watch a film, or check tomorrow's snow and weather report while they get ready for dinner. It turns dead time into pleasure time, and it does so without asking the guest to learn anything new — it is simply a TV, in the room where they spend their most relaxed hour.

The technology behind the experience

Bathroom electronics live in a hostile environment: steam, splashing water, rapid temperature changes. A mirror TV built for this setting needs proper moisture engineering, not just a pretty frame. Oolaa is a bathroom TV specialist — the company is the market leader in Hong Kong bathroom TV and kitchen TV, with 31,000+ installations across Hong Kong and Macau — and its bathroom products are built to IP65, a higher protection rating than the IPX4 common in the category, with a built-in defogger so the picture stays clear even in a steamy bathroom.

The "No Login. Just Cast." philosophy of Oolaa's flagship smart mirror TV range matters here too. Hotel guests do not want to log in to anything, remember passwords, or pair devices through fiddly menus. Casting content straight to the mirror TV keeps the experience effortless — which is exactly what a five-star stay should feel like.

What hotel operators should know before specifying mirror TVs

If you run a hotel and are considering mirror TVs, Park Hyatt Niseko's example offers a few lessons. First, go for consistency: installing the mirror TV in every bathroom, as Niseko did, means every guest gets the same experience and your housekeeping and maintenance teams deal with one standard product. Second, check the moisture rating and defogger — a bathroom TV without proper steam protection will disappoint within a season. Third, think about the content experience: casting support and a simple interface matter more than a long feature list, because guests will not read a manual.

Finally, work with a supplier that understands hospitality at scale.
