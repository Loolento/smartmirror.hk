Royal Garden Hotel Adds Oolaa Mirror TV

March 17, 2025

Oolaa has installed its state-of-the-art Mirror TV at the Royal Garden Hotel in Hong Kong. This innovative feature enhances the hotel's luxury bathrooms, merging entertainment with style, and elevates the smart living experience for guests at this premier luxury hotel.

When the mirror is the television

A Mirror TV does the job of two fixtures at once. It is a mirror — and it is also a television. In a hotel bathroom, that dual identity matters: guests get entertainment without the visual clutter of a separate screen competing with the room's design. The fixture belongs in the bathroom first, and entertains second, which is exactly the order luxury guests expect.

For the hotel, the gain is twofold. The bathroom keeps its clean, composed look, and the guest gets a smarter stay: news, weather and entertainment woven into the morning and evening routine, in the room where those routines happen.

Why luxury hotels are putting screens in the bathroom

The bathroom has quietly become one of the most competitive rooms in a luxury hotel. Guests may forget a lobby, but they remember how a bathroom felt at 7 a.m. Properties are responding by upgrading the room's technology — and entertainment is high on the list, because it is used every single day of a stay.

The buying question for hotel teams is rarely whether to add a screen, but how to add one without cheapening the room. A Mirror TV answers that by merging the screen into a fixture the bathroom needs anyway. Specifiers typically weigh the same points: Does it preserve the design language of the bathroom? Is it built for a wet environment? And does it elevate the guest's experience, or merely add a gadget?

Smart living, extended to the guest room

At the Royal Garden Hotel, the Mirror TV lifts the smart living experience beyond the hotel's public spaces and into the most private room of the suite. It is the same philosophy Oolaa applies in residences — technology that blends into the room and the routine — carried into a hospitality setting where the audience changes every few days and first impressions are everything.

That work sits within Oolaa's core specialty. The company is Hong Kong's market leader in bathroom TV and kitchen TV, factory-direct from its own Shenzhen plant, with more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau — experience that shows up in projects from luxury residences to premier hotels like the Royal Garden.
