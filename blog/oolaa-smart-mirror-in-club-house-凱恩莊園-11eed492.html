Oolaa Smart Mirror in Manor Hill Clubhouse

March 17, 2023

Our company supplied the Oolaa Smart Mirror — a piece of cutting-edge technology — to the luxurious Manor Hill club house in Tseung Kwan O, Hong Kong. In the clubhouse gym, the mirror has a very specific job: it is an innovative crossover with KaraMirror, bringing a comprehensive fitness program to all residents.

A fitness mirror for the whole clubhouse

A residential clubhouse serves everyone in the development, and its equipment has to work for all of them — the early riser who trains before work, the beginner who needs guidance, the resident who simply wants a guided session without booking a personal trainer. A fitness mirror answers that brief in a single surface: when it is off, it is an elegant mirror on the gym wall; when it is on, it becomes the instructor.

Through the KaraMirror crossover, the Oolaa Smart Mirror offers a comprehensive fitness program for all residents. Guided training lives in the mirror itself, so a workout does not depend on class timetables — residents can train when the clubhouse fits their day, following a program on the glass in front of them.

Why developers put fitness mirrors in clubhouses

For a development, the clubhouse is an amenity residents judge daily. A fitness mirror upgrades the gym without expanding it: one installation adds guided training capacity to the room, keeps the space visually clean, and gives the fitness offer a modern, premium feel that matches the rest of the clubhouse. It is the same logic Oolaa applies across its work — technology that serves a real routine, presented as furniture rather than equipment.

At Manor Hill, that means residents of a luxury Tseung Kwan O development have a comprehensive fitness program waiting for them downstairs — on a mirror.
