HSITP Dorms Get Oolaa Smart Mirrors at the Foyer

February 04, 2025

The Hong Kong-Shenzhen Innovation and Technology Park (HSITP) is a pivotal development in Hong Kong's innovation and technology sector, boasting a size three times larger than the current Hong Kong Science Park. As the park takes shape, Oolaa is getting ready to be part of daily life there: we will equip each dormitory with a smart mirror at the foyer.

A smart mirror at every foyer

The foyer is the natural control point of a home — the first place you reach when you walk in, and the last place you pass on your way out. Placing the smart mirror there means residents can seamlessly manage their smart home features from the mirror itself, as part of arriving and leaving. It is the future of hi-tech living: cutting-edge solutions for a truly connected lifestyle, built into the doorway of every dormitory home.

What foyer control means

A foyer mirror is more than a screen by the door. In an Oolaa smart home, the foyer mirror works as a whole-home hub: through Google Assistant, Bluetooth and Zigbee it ties the home's smart devices into one point of control, and via Matter it works with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa and Xiaomi. For residents, the experience is simple — the home answers at the door, in one place, without a pocketful of remotes or a phone in hand.

Getting ready

At the time of writing, both sides of the project are in motion: the construction site of HSITP is rising, and the Oolaa smart mirrors for the dormitories are under production in our own factory in Shenzhen. Experience the future of hi-tech living — it is being built now.
