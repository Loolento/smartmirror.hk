Victoria Coast: AI Smart Mirror Lobbies, Pok Fu Lam

Sep 18, 2023

Chinachem has built Victoria Coast, an ultra-luxury new development in Pok Fu Lam, Hong Kong, filled with artificial intelligence. Every residence is configured with smart home technology — and Oolaa smart mirrors were introduced into the lobby of every tower.

Victoria Coast: luxury, intelligence everywhere

Victoria Coast is Chinachem's statement on the Pok Fu Lam waterfront: an extremely luxurious new property where artificial intelligence is not a show-flat gimmick but part of the development's fabric. Beyond the architecture and the finishes, the project's defining feature is that every household is configured with a smart home system as standard.

That whole-development commitment is what makes the project notable. When intelligence is specified for every unit rather than offered as an upgrade, the technology has to be genuinely livable — reliable enough for daily use, simple enough for every resident, and consistent enough to feel like part of the building rather than an add-on.

Smart mirrors in every tower lobby

Oolaa smart mirrors were installed in the lobby of each tower at Victoria Coast — the shared threshold every resident passes daily. The lobby is a shrewd placement: it is the one location in a residential development with guaranteed foot traffic, dwell time and attention, twice a day, for every household.

Unlike a printed notice board that residents learn to ignore, a smart mirror earns glances because it is also a mirror — people look at it while waiting for the lift. That built-in attention makes the lobby mirror the development's most effective communication surface: announcements, management notices and community information reach residents at the exact moment they are standing still with thirty seconds to spare.

Cantonese AI conversation with residents

These are not silent displays. The Victoria Coast smart mirrors converse with residents in precise Cantonese voice and use artificial intelligence to provide accurate, useful information — a genuinely convenient living experience rather than a digital poster.

Voice interaction in the resident's mother language is what makes the lobby mirror approachable across generations. A retiree who would never tap through a residents' app can simply speak; a busy professional can ask while walking past. AI-backed answers mean the mirror can handle real questions, not just play a loop — turning the lobby from a pass-through space into a serviced one.

What developers can take from this project

Victoria Coast demonstrates a layered smart-development strategy worth studying. Layer one: smart home in every unit, so intelligence is the baseline, not the upgrade. Layer two: shared intelligent surfaces — the Oolaa lobby mirrors — that serve the whole community and keep the development's communication channel alive after handover.

For developers planning the next luxury project, the lesson is about placement as much as product. In-unit technology impresses buyers on the show-flat tour; lobby technology serves residents every day for years, and it is visible to every visitor, every agent and every prospective buyer who walks through. A tower lobby with an AI mirror that speaks Cantonese tells a stronger story about a development's intelligence than any brochure — because the visitor experiences it rather than reading about it. That is the standard Chinachem set at Victoria Coast, and the playbook is available to any developer ready to specify it.

And the economics favor it: one well-placed lobby mirror serves hundreds of households for years, updating its content centrally as the community evolves — new management notices, seasonal greetings, emergency announcements. Compared with printed materials that age on the wall, the mirror stays current, stays useful, and keeps telling the development's intelligence story long after the sales office has closed.
