Victoria Coast: AI Smart Mirror Lobbies, Pok Fu Lam

Sep 18, 2023

Chinachem has built an extremely luxurious new development in Pok Fu Lam, Hong Kong — Victoria Coast — and filled it with artificial intelligence. Every unit comes equipped with smart home technology, and the experience begins before residents even reach their front door: Oolaa smart mirrors are installed in every tower lobby.

A smart mirror in every lobby

The lobby is where a development makes its first impression, every single day. At Victoria Coast, that impression includes a smart mirror — a familiar, elegant surface that is also an intelligent point of contact for residents. Placing the mirrors in every lobby means the service is not a showroom extra in one tower; it is part of the standard of the whole development, available to every resident on every trip in and out.

It is a deliberate choice about where intelligence belongs in a building: not hidden in a plant room or locked inside an app, but on the wall, in the space residents pass through daily.

Cantonese conversation, AI answers

These intelligent mirrors engage in precise Cantonese voice conversation. That precision matters in Hong Kong: a lobby assistant that truly understands Cantonese can be used by every generation of a family, in the language they speak at home, without translation or hesitation. Oolaa's voice control runs through Google Assistant with Cantonese support, which is why the conversation feels natural rather than scripted.

Through artificial intelligence, the mirrors provide residents with accurate and efficient information, allowing them to enjoy a more convenient living experience. Questions that once meant finding a concierge or opening an app can be asked out loud, in passing — and answered on the spot.

Part of a fully smart development

The lobby mirrors are one layer of a development-wide approach: with each unit equipped with smart home technology, Victoria Coast treats intelligence as infrastructure, not decoration. The Oolaa smart mirror fits that philosophy — a piece of furniture-grade design on the surface, and a working part of the building's service underneath.

For developers watching how luxury projects differentiate, Victoria Coast shows the pattern: put capable, Cantonese-speaking AI where residents actually meet the building — in the lobby — and let convenience become part of the address.
