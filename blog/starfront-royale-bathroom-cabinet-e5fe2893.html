Starfront Royale: Luxury Smart Mirror Bathroom

Sep 18, 2026

Starfront Royale is a new residential development in Hong Kong — and in its bathrooms, the mirror is far more than a mirror. Oolaa Smart Mirror supplied a luxury smart mirror bathroom for the project, designed together with the interior designers so the technology sits naturally inside a high-end scheme. Take a look at the finished bathroom in the video above.

What the smart mirror does

The mirror keeps the information a resident actually needs in front of them: it displays the time, the weather, music and even body weight — the small checks that start and end every day, delivered at a glance while getting ready. The mirror is AI-powered and connects with both iOS and Google smart home systems, so it fits into the way the household already runs its devices rather than asking anyone to learn a new one.

That combination — everyday information, entertainment and smart-home connection in a single surface — is what turns a bathroom fitting into a daily convenience. There is no phone to prop up on the counter and no separate speaker or scale display competing for space: the mirror handles it.

One design, not separate pieces

The Starfront Royale bathroom was designed as a single composition. The smart mirror, the vanity and a Dyson hairdryer magnetic mount were designed together as one unit, so storage, styling tools and technology arrive in a single, resolved piece of furniture rather than a collection of add-ons.

The materials carry the same intent: a matte gold frame set against dark wood cabinetry and a marble countertop. The gold is deliberately understated — a warm metallic accent that suits the dark timber and stone — and because the mirror was designed with the interior designers from the start, its frame, proportions and finish belong to the room instead of being applied to it afterwards.

The bathroom around it

The rest of the bathroom holds the same standard: a smart toilet, a glass shower, and gold detailing carried throughout the room. The smart mirror is the technological centrepiece, but it works because everything around it was specified to the same level — the mirror's matte gold frame echoes the detailing, and the dark wood and marble give the technology a calm, residential backdrop.

For developers and designers, this is the pattern that matters: the smart mirror was not a late substitution for a standard mirror. It was part of the bathroom design conversation, which is why the vanity, the hairdryer mount and the mirror read as one idea.
