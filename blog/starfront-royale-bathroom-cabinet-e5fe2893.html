Starfront Royale Bathroom | Oolaa Smart Mirror Cabinet

Sep 18, 2026

Oolaa Smart Mirror supplies a luxury smart mirror bathroom for Starfront Royale (帝御•星濤), a new residential development in Hong Kong, designed together with the interior designers.

What can the smart mirror do?

• Displays time, weather, music and body weight
• Matte gold frame with dark wood cabinetry and marble countertop
• AI-powered, connects with iOS and Google smart home

How is the bathroom integrated?

• Smart mirror, vanity and Dyson hairdryer magnetic mount in one design
• Smart toilet, glass shower and gold detailing throughout
