Oolaa Smart Mirror Foyer at Henderson Artlane No.2

Apr 27, 2023

Welcome to the Oolaa smart mirror — a high-tech smart home product designed for modern luxury homes. At Artlane No.2, a residential project by Henderson Land, the Oolaa smart mirror takes its place at the foyer, where it becomes the home's convenience assistant: the first thing that greets you at the door, and the surface that runs the home behind it.

Connected to Yoswit and Google AI

The Oolaa smart mirror works with Henderson Land's own Yoswit smart home system and with Google's AI, combining them to deliver efficient home control and an intelligent living experience. That integration is the point of the foyer position: instead of a mirror that merely sits in the home's systems, it stands at the entrance as a working control point for them — efficient to use precisely because it is where residents naturally pass, pause and prepare to leave or arrive.

Touch, voice and phone — every way to control

Through its touchscreen, the mirror can browse the web, check the weather, set schedules, play music and control smart home appliances. It fully supports voice control and mobile app operation as well, so residents can run every smart device in the home in whichever way suits the moment — a tap on the glass, a spoken command, or the phone in their pocket.

That flexibility is what makes the mirror a genuine assistant rather than a novelty. The weather check happens while you are choosing shoes; the schedule is reviewed where the day actually starts; the home can be set for leaving, or readied for arrival, from the same spot.

An air monitor on the wall

The Oolaa smart mirror also carries an air monitor, tracking indoor air quality in real time. When the air needs attention, it can automatically activate the air purifier — so the family keeps breathing the healthiest air without anyone having to watch a gauge or remember to switch a machine on. It is a quiet kind of intelligence: the home notices, and the home acts.

Designed to belong in a luxury home

The mirror's design blends perfectly with modern luxury homes. It provides practical function, but it is also a high-end, fashionable decorative piece — a bright feature in the home rather than a gadget to be hidden. Modern life is inseparable from smart technology, and at Artlane No.2 the Oolaa smart mirror shows what that means at the front door: an important member of the household, bringing a smarter living experience from the moment you walk in.

Key facts

  • Project: Artlane No.2, a residential project by Henderson Land; the Oolaa smart mirror is installed at the foyer.
  • The mirror integrates with Henderson Land's Yoswit smart home system and Google AI for home control.
