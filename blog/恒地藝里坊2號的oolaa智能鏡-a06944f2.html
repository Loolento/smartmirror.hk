Oolaa Smart Mirror Foyer at Henderson Artlane No.2

Apr 27, 2023

The Oolaa smart mirror is a high-tech smart home product designed for the modern luxury residence. At Henderson's Artlane No.2 residential project, it takes pride of place in the foyer — becoming the household's everyday living assistant from the moment you walk through the door.

The foyer: the smart home's command center

Placement is strategy. The foyer is the one space every resident passes at least twice a day — leaving in the morning, returning at night — which makes it the natural command center for the connected home. A smart mirror here is glanceable on the way out (weather, schedule, home status) and welcoming on the way in.

Oolaa's Foyer mirror is designed for exactly this role: a whole-home control hub reachable via Google Assistant, Bluetooth and Zigbee. At Artlane No.2, the foyer mirror greets the household's comings and goings with information and control in one surface — the last thing you check before leaving and the first thing that welcomes you home.

Working with Yoswit and Google AI

The Artlane No.2 installation integrates with Henderson's Yoswit smart home system as well as Google's AI platform, delivering efficient home control and an intelligent living experience. This dual integration matters: Yoswit provides the development-grade device ecosystem, while Google AI brings world-class voice intelligence and services.

For residents, the practical result is choice. Control the home by touching the mirror's screen, by speaking — with full voice control supported — or through the mobile app. Different household members prefer different interfaces; a well-designed smart home accommodates all of them instead of forcing one. And because the integration spans established platforms rather than a closed silo, the system remains extensible as the household's needs evolve.

One mirror, many jobs

The Oolaa smart mirror's touchscreen handles an impressive range: browse the web, check the weather, set schedules, play music and control smart home appliances. Morning routines compress into a single glance — today's weather and calendar while the coffee brews, music for the commute, a final check that the appliances are as they should be. Evenings work the same way in reverse: arriving home to a mirror that already knows the day's schedule makes winding down feel effortless.

Then there is the air quality monitor on board. The mirror continuously monitors indoor air quality and can automatically activate the air purifier when needed, so the family always breathes the healthiest air. This is the quiet kind of intelligence that defines a mature smart home: not a feature you show off, but a guardian you forget is there — until you notice how rarely you think about it.

Lessons for luxury residential projects

Artlane No.2 illustrates three principles developers should note. First, put the intelligence where life happens: the foyer mirror gets used because it sits on the path of daily life, not in a corner that requires a special trip. Second, integrate with the development's chosen platform — here Yoswit — plus a global AI layer, so residents get both local reliability and world-class capability. Third, include ambient intelligence like air quality automation, which delivers daily value without demanding daily attention.

The result is a residence where technology feels less like equipment and more like staff: present, capable and unobtrusive.
