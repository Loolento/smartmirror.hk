Heartland Residence: 60 Oolaa Mirror TVs Installed

Mar 26, 2025

Sixty master bathrooms at Heartland Residence in Wuhan now begin and end the day with a screen in the mirror. Oolaa supplied 60 sets of its luxury Mirror TVs for the development's master bathrooms, giving residents a first-class smart entertainment experience in the one room where a conventional television never belonged.

Entertainment where the day starts and ends

A mirror TV is exactly what it sounds like: a television built into the mirror. Off, it is a clean mirror surface that suits a luxury bathroom. On, it becomes a screen — news and weather with the morning routine, a show or a match during an evening soak. For the master bathroom, that turns dead time into lived time: the minutes spent washing up, getting ready, and winding down become the most informed, most relaxing minutes of the day.

At Heartland Residence, this was not a one-off showpiece. Sixty sets were installed across the development's master bathrooms, so the experience is a standard of the home, not a lucky upgrade for a few units.

Why bathroom TVs are an Oolaa specialty

Bathroom screens are where Oolaa built its name. Oolaa (Viewdex Limited) is a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen, and it is the market leader in bathroom TVs and kitchen TVs in Hong Kong, with more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau. A bathroom is the harshest room in the home for electronics — steam, splashes, daily temperature swings — and two decades of bathroom installations is exactly the experience a developer is buying when the mirror has to work flawlessly in every one of 60 bathrooms, for years.

What developers should check in a bathroom mirror TV

If you are specifying bathroom screens for a project, three checks matter most. First, the mirror itself: when the TV is off, the surface should still read as a proper mirror, not a dark grey panel. Second, consistency at volume: one beautiful unit in a showroom means little if unit sixty varies from unit one, so ask how the supplier controls quality across a bulk order. Third, the supplier's bathroom track record — how many bathrooms they have actually delivered, and whether they manufacture themselves or resell someone else's hardware. Elegance and technology blend perfectly only when all three answers are solid.
