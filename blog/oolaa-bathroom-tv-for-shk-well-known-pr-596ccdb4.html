Oolaa Bathroom TV in SHK's The Cullinan, Hong Kong

Mar 22, 2023

SHK featured our Oolaa bathroom TV prominently in its renowned project, The Cullinan, at Kowloon Station. Equipped with a waterproof design and a built-in DMB digital tuner, the bathroom TV is a fitting addition to a luxurious bathroom — and a telling choice by one of Hong Kong's most exacting developers.

Featured by SHK, in a flagship address

The Cullinan is among the best-known luxury residential names in Hong Kong, and developers of SHK's standing do not feature products prominently by accident. Fixtures in a show bathroom at this level are chosen the way everything else in the project is chosen: to set a standard that buyers will measure other developments against.

That the bathroom TV earned a prominent place in that setting says something about where bathroom entertainment now sits in luxury residential design — not as a novelty, but as an expected part of a complete bathroom.

Waterproof, with a built-in DMB tuner

Two specifications define the product. The first is its waterproof design: a bathroom is a wet, steamy environment, and any screen installed there has to be designed for that environment from the start, not adapted to it afterwards. Waterproofing is not a bonus feature in a bathroom TV; it is the entry ticket.

The second is the built-in DMB digital tuner. DMB is a digital broadcast standard, and building the tuner into the television means broadcast channels are received by the TV itself — no separate box to house, hide or wire in the bathroom. For the viewer, the experience is simple: television, in the bath, as it should be.

For developers and designers specifying bathrooms, the Cullinan example carries a practical lesson: in a true luxury bathroom, entertainment is no longer an afterthought added by the owner with a tablet propped on the counter. It is specified, integrated and waterproofed at the design stage — and the projects that treat it that way are the ones buyers remember.

From Hong Kong's bathroom TV specialists

Bathroom TV is Oolaa's home ground. The company is the market leader in bathroom TV and kitchen TV in Hong Kong, factory-direct from its own plant in Shenzhen, with more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau. Its bathroom televisions have been supplied to SHK projects — and in The Cullinan, SHK chose to feature the product prominently, which remains the clearest endorsement a specification can get.
