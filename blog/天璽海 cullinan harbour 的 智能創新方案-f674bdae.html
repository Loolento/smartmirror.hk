Cullinan Harbour: Smart Switch with Voice Feedback

Oct 21, 2025

Cullinan Harbour, developed by Sun Hung Kai Properties, is the super-luxury new landmark of the Kai Tak runway area. For this prestigious project, Oolaa developed an exclusive voice-chip smart solution that transforms the everyday wireless switch — with instant Power On/Off sound feedback that makes control precise and reliable.

Cullinan Harbour: the Kai Tak runway's super-luxury landmark

Cullinan Harbour stands on the former Kai Tak runway — one of Hong Kong's most symbolically charged pieces of land, now reborn as a waterfront luxury district. As Sun Hung Kai Properties' flagship address there, the development carries the expectations that come with the name: architecture, finishes and technology all held to the standard of Hong Kong's top-tier residential market.

In that context, the smart home cannot be an afterthought or a box-ticking exercise. Buyers at this level have lived with technology all their lives; they notice when it works beautifully and they notice — unforgivingly — when it does not. Every interaction, down to the humble light switch, has to feel considered.

The overlooked problem: silent wireless switches

Wireless switches are a staple of modern smart homes — they can be placed anywhere, they control scenes, and they avoid rewiring. But traditional wireless switches share a subtle flaw: silence. Press one, and nothing confirms the command was received. Did the light respond? Is the press still traveling through the network? In a large luxury residence, where the controlled device may be rooms away, that uncertainty is a daily friction.

Residents adapt by pressing again, walking to check, or simply losing trust in the system. For a super-luxury development, 'press and hope' is not an acceptable user experience — it is precisely the kind of small imperfection that erodes confidence in the entire smart home.

Oolaa's answer: a voice chip with instant audio feedback

For Cullinan Harbour, Oolaa engineered an exclusive voice-chip smart solution that fundamentally improves the traditional wireless switch experience. When a resident successfully powers a device on or off, the system instantly emits Power On/Off sound feedback — an audible confirmation that the command landed.

The effect is disproportionate to its simplicity. Sound closes the feedback loop that wireless control broke: press, hear, trust. Control becomes precise because every action is acknowledged; it becomes reliable because residents stop second-guessing. It is a small innovation aimed at exactly the right pain point — the kind of detail that distinguishes a genuinely thoughtful smart home from a collection of connected devices. In a development of Cullinan Harbour's standing, where every finish is scrutinized, that difference is felt daily — and remembered at resale.

Smart home meets IoT, at luxury standard

The voice-chip solution combines smart home technology with Internet of Things engineering, and it exemplifies what Hong Kong's top residential market now demands: technology that pursues both intelligence and comfort to the extreme.

For developers, the broader lesson from Cullinan Harbour is about where to invest engineering attention. The industry obsesses over headline features — whole-home scenes, app dashboards, voice assistants — while residents' actual satisfaction often hinges on micro-interactions: the switch that confirms, the mirror that greets, the system that never leaves you wondering. Oolaa's approach, refined across 31,000+ installations in Hong Kong and Macau, is to engineer those micro-interactions deliberately — and to build custom solutions, like this voice-chip switch, when a landmark project deserves more than the catalog.
