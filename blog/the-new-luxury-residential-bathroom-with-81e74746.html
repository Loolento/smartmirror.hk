Luxury Residential Bathroom with Smart Mirror

April 15, 2023

One of our recent job references: an Oolaa Smart Mirror installed in the bathroom of a luxury Hong Kong residence. The project is The Knightsbridge, and the bathroom shows why the smart mirror has become standard equipment in high-end homes — it is the one screen that belongs in the room without ever looking like one. For full project information, see The Knightsbridge website.

Why the bathroom is the smart mirror's home ground

Everyone starts the day in front of the bathroom mirror, and that routine is full of small questions: the time, the weather, the schedule, the news. A smart mirror answers them on the glass you are already looking at. Oolaa's bathroom mirrors are built for the room's realities — rated IP65 against the moisture in the air, with a built-in defogger so the surface clears after a hot shower, and motion detection that wakes the display as you approach. The mirror behaves like a mirror first, and like a screen exactly when you need one.

There is also a point of trust in a job reference that no rendering can offer: this is a real bathroom, in a real residence, fitted and finished. Developers and homeowners touring a completed project can stand in front of the mirror, watch the display wake as they approach, and judge the reflection with the screen off — the two states every buyer should check, in person, before specifying anything.

What to specify for a bathroom smart mirror

If you are choosing a bathroom smart mirror for a residence, four points decide whether you will still be happy in five years. First, protection: steam is relentless, so check the waterproof rating before anything else. Second, the mirror quality with the screen off — the reflection is what you will see most of the day. Third, defogging: a mirror that fogs is a mirror that fails at its first job. Fourth, the supplier: a company that manufactures its own mirrors and has thousands of bathrooms behind it will still be there when you need support.

Oolaa (Viewdex Limited) is a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen and more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau — and as Hong Kong's market leader in bathroom TVs, bathroom screens are the company's home ground. This Knightsbridge bathroom is one reference among many; the standard it was built to is the same one applied to every project.
