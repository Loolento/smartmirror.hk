Waterproof Bathroom TV: It's Never Too Late

August 12, 2022

I have a vivid memory from childhood: my parents would send me to take a shower during the commercial breaks of a TV program, and every single time, I would bathe in a rush. The show would come back on without me, and the bath was measured not in minutes but in ad slots.

The commercial-break bath

That was simply how it worked then. The television lived in the living room, the bathroom had no screen, and if you wanted to watch, you washed fast. Nobody thought of it as a problem to be solved — it was just the geometry of the house. The program's schedule set the bath's schedule.

What has changed is not the desire. Everyone still wants to keep watching while the water runs. What has changed is that the screen can finally go where the bath is.

Technology improves life — bath time included

Science and technology are improving our lives in ways large and small, and the bathroom is no exception. A waterproof TV can be installed in the bathroom itself, designed for the wet environment — so why not watch a TV program while enjoying the bath moment?

The difference is bigger than convenience. An unhurried bath is a genuinely different experience from a rushed one: the program ends when it ends, the soak lasts as long as it should, and one of the few quiet moments of the day stops being timed by somebody else's broadcast schedule.

Why "never too late" is the point

Homeowners often assume bathroom entertainment is a decision made when a home is built or renovated — miss that moment, and the option is gone. That assumption is exactly what the title of this post argues with. A waterproof bathroom TV is an installation, not a rebuild: finished bathrooms can gain one too.

As general buying guidance, the points worth considering are straightforward: the viewing position from the tub, a design made for waterproof bathroom use, and installation by people who understand the product. Get those right, and a bathroom that has been finished for years can still get its upgrade. It is, as we say, never too late.
