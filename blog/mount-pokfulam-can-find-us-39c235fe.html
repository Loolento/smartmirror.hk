Oolaa Smart Mirror Inside Mount PokFuLam Homes

February 3, 2025

We are involved in some of the most luxurious projects in Hong Kong — among them Mount PokFuLam, developed by Wang On Properties, where each residence is priced at a minimum of US$40 million. At that level, every fixture in the home is a deliberate choice, and the master bathroom is no exception.

The master bathroom's centerpiece

Each master bathroom at Mount PokFuLam features the innovative Oolaa Smart Mirror, with a unique and flamboyant design that truly steals the spotlight. In a residence of this calibre, the mirror is not an afterthought hung on a wall — it is a statement piece, sized and styled to hold its own against marble, brass and everything else competing for attention.

The photographs in this post show the mirrors in place: the Oolaa Smart Mirror in the master bathroom, its inner appearance, and further master bathroom and bathroom designs across the residences. Each installation is tailored to its room, which is what bespoke luxury actually means in practice.

Entertainment for music lovers

The Oolaa Smart Mirror offers a hi-tech entertainment experience, integrating seamlessly with platforms like Spotify and YouTube. For music lovers, that makes it an ideal addition: the morning routine comes with a soundtrack, the evening wind-down with a video, and none of it requires a separate speaker or a phone propped against the taps.

It is worth pausing on why this matters in a luxury home. The technology residents use most is rarely the most exotic — it is the technology that fits the moments they repeat every day. A mirror that handles music and video at the sink fits the most repeated moment of all.

Nor is one mirror the whole story. A residence at this level has more than one bathroom, and the project photographs show the mirrors across several of them — each room with its own proportions and finishes. Consistency of experience, tailored room by room, is harder to deliver than a single spectacular installation, and it is what owners actually live with.

Service, and the wider smart home

With exceptional customer service behind it, this smart solution enhances the home automation experience for discerning homeowners seeking to elevate their luxury smart home environment. A showpiece that is not properly supported is just a risk; at this end of the market, the product and the service are bought together.

Experience the future of entertainment and innovation with Oolaa — at Mount PokFuLam, that future is already installed, one master bathroom at a time.
