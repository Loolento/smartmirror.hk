One Chancery: Oolaa Smart Mirrors in Luxury Suites

Sep 18, 2026

One Chancery is a new luxury service apartment at 1 Chancery Lane, Central, developed by China Resources Land and set beside Tai Kwun, with accommodation ranging from studios to a four-bedroom penthouse. Oolaa Smart Mirror worked with the project's interior designers to craft the smart mirror and vanity configuration installed in every bathroom across the building. From the smallest studio to the penthouse, every guest is greeted by the same hotel-grade detail: a rounded backlit smart mirror displaying time, weather and digital information.

What defines the One Chancery bathrooms

The One Chancery bathrooms were designed around a calm palette of precious materials. White marble walls with grey veining set the tone, matched by a gold showerhead, gold tap and gold frame that lift the room into true hotel grade. The glass shower enclosure carries the same gold trim, so the whole wet area reads as one composed design statement rather than a collection of fixtures.

The centrepiece is a rounded backlit smart mirror. The soft curve breaks the rectilinear lines of the marble, while the backlight gives the vanity area a warm, even glow that flatters the face far better than a single overhead downlight. Because the mirror is also a display, it shows time, weather and digital information at a glance — a small touch that changes the rhythm of a morning in a service apartment, where guests are often planning their day while they get ready.

Why luxury service apartments are specifying smart mirrors

Central's luxury service-apartment market is fiercely competitive. Guests choosing between addresses are buying an experience, not just square footage, and bathrooms are where that experience is judged most honestly. A smart mirror is one of the few upgrades a guest notices within seconds of entering the room — it is interactive, it is visibly different, and it photographs well for the listings that drive bookings.

For the operator, consistency matters as much as wow factor. One Chancery runs from studios to a four-bedroom penthouse, and the smart mirror and vanity configuration is integrated across all units. That means one specification, one installation standard and one maintenance story for the whole building, instead of a patchwork of different bathroom packages. When every bathroom carries the same intelligent mirror, the brand promise of the property holds up in every room category.

How the Oolaa smart mirror works in a hospitality setting

Each One Chancery mirror works with iOS and Google smart home platforms and is powered by AI. In practice that means the mirror behaves as part of the room's connected ecosystem rather than as a standalone gadget: the display layer presents time, weather and information without the guest touching anything, and the underlying platform can integrate with the smart-home controls a property chooses to offer.

For a service apartment, this platform approach has a practical payoff. Information that used to live on printed cards — weather, time, notices — can live on the mirror surface instead, always current and never needing reprinting. And because the mirror is AI-powered and platform-compatible, the property is not locked into a single vendor's app: the installation grows with the smart-home standards the industry is converging on, including Matter, which Oolaa supports across its smart-home range.

A buyer's guide: what to specify in a smart bathroom mirror

Developers and interior designers specifying smart mirrors for a luxury project should look past the novelty and check the fundamentals. First, light quality: a backlit mirror should give even, shadow-free illumination for grooming, not just a decorative halo. Second, moisture engineering: a bathroom mirror lives in steam, so look for a properly rated product — Oolaa's bathroom mirrors are built to IP65, a higher rating than the IPX4 common in the category, and include a built-in defogger and motion detection. Third, the display: time, weather and information should be legible at vanity distance without dominating the mirror.

Fourth, platform compatibility: confirm the mirror works with the smart-home ecosystems your project uses — Oolaa mirrors work with iOS and Google smart home, and the company's smart-home products support Matter, Zigbee, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and KNX, connecting through to Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa and Xiaomi. Finally, think about the vanity as a configuration, not a product. At One Chancery, Oolaa worked with the interior designers on the smart mirror and vanity configuration together — mirror, lighting, and how the unit meets the marble and the gold detailing. That coordination is what makes the difference between a gadget stuck on a wall and a bathroom that feels designed.
