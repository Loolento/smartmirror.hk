Oolaa Smart Kitchen Device: Built for the Kitchen

October 5, 2026

The kitchen is the most lived-in room in any home — and the hardest place for technology to fit in. Oolaa's new Smart Kitchen Device was designed for kitchen use from the start, with every part of how it works shaped around the way people actually cook. The aim is simple: cooking that is happier, easier, and more convenient.

See it in action in the demo above: mid-recipe, the cook pulls up a tiramisu video on YouTube — no phone to unlock, no screen to wipe clean, and the cooking never stops.

Born for the kitchen

Most smart devices on the market were designed for the living room or the bedroom; very few truly consider how a kitchen is used. The Oolaa Smart Kitchen Device was different from the first day of the project: it focused on one thing only — the kitchen. That scenario-first focus is something other brands simply do not have.

Designed around the cooking flow

From prep to plating, cooking runs in one continuous motion, and controls should never become an obstacle in the middle of it. Every operation detail of the Smart Kitchen Device was polished against real kitchen scenarios, so the cook stays composed even when things get busy. In practice, that means the everyday routine a cook actually needs: a recipe video on YouTube, a countdown timer set by voice, a TV app running while dinner comes together.

Happy, joyful, convenient

The ultimate purpose of technology is to make people happier. At Oolaa, we believe good technology should never ask people to adapt to it — it should blend into life on its own. That is the design philosophy of the Smart Kitchen Device: joyful to use, a pleasure to live with, and convenient where it counts.

The newest piece of Oolaa's smart living map

From smart mirrors to smart kitchen devices, Oolaa is bringing AI technology into every corner of the home. The Smart Kitchen Device is the newest piece of that puzzle — and our latest interpretation of what "smart living" means.

Why choose Oolaa

Three things set the Smart Kitchen Device apart: innovation focused on the kitchen scenario; an operating experience designed around the user's happiness; and a place in Oolaa's growing smart ecosystem. Behind it stands Oolaa (Viewdex Limited), a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen and more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau. High technology, put to work cooking up a happier life.

  • The Oolaa Smart Kitchen Device is a smart device designed specifically for kitchen usage scenarios — not a living-room product adapted for the kitchen.
