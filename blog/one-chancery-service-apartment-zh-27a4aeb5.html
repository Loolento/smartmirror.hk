One Chancery: Oolaa Smart Mirrors in Every Bathroom

September 18, 2026

One Chancery is China Resources Land’s new luxury serviced residence in the heart of Central — and Oolaa Smart Mirror is part of its story. Set on Shing Shan Lane, moments from Tai Kwun and SoHo, the residence spans units from open-plan layouts to four-bedroom duplexes. Working side by side with the interior designers, the Oolaa team designed the smart mirror and vanity configuration for every single bathroom, so the technology feels designed-in rather than added-on.

One Chancery: Central’s New Serviced Residence

Location is the first luxury, and One Chancery has it: Shing Shan Lane in Central, one of Hong Kong’s most storied neighbourhoods, with Tai Kwun’s heritage courtyards and SoHo’s dining streets a short stroll away. As a brand-new serviced residence from an established developer, it is aimed squarely at executives and families who want Central living with hotel-grade service — a segment where expectations are exacting and first impressions are everything.

The unit mix tells you who it is for: from efficient open-plan units for the solo executive to four-bedroom duplexes for relocating families. That range creates a design challenge — how do you keep the experience consistently luxurious from the smallest unit to the largest? One Chancery’s answer includes standardising the bathroom experience, with an Oolaa smart mirror configured for every bathroom in every unit type. Consistency, executed well, is itself a luxury.

Designed Together: Oolaa and the Interior Designers

The part of this project we are proudest of is the process. Rather than supplying mirrors to a specification written in isolation, the Oolaa team worked directly with the interior designers to shape each bathroom’s mirror and vanity configuration — dimensions, positioning, sightlines, and how the mirror’s display integrates with the vanity lighting and storage around it.

This collaboration matters more than it sounds. A smart mirror is both a reflective surface and a display, so its height, size, and relationship to the basin and lighting determine whether it feels like part of the architecture or an appliance on the wall. Getting those decisions right on paper — before a single unit is fitted — is what separates a coordinated result from a collection of parts. For developers and designers reading this: bring the technology partner into the design conversation early. It costs nothing and changes everything.

The Bathroom Details: Marble, Gold, and a Glowing Round Mirror

Step into a One Chancery bathroom and the design language is immediately clear: white marble walls with grey veining, gold shower heads, gold faucets, and a gold mirror frame — a palette that reads as modern classic rather than trendy. The glass shower enclosure is trimmed in gold, giving the whole room a hotel-grade sense of occasion.

At the centre of it all is a round, backlit smart mirror. The circular form softens the room’s geometry, the backlighting flatters the face far better than a harsh downlight, and the mirror surface itself displays the time, weather, and digital information at a glance. It is the room’s jewellery and its information hub in one object — beautiful when idle, useful from the first minute of the day. The gold frame ties it visually to the taps and shower fittings, so the technology never looks like an afterthought.

Details like these are why the designer collaboration mattered: the mirror’s diameter, the warmth of its backlight, and the information layout on the glass were all tuned to this specific bathroom — not pulled from a catalogue. The result photographs beautifully, but more importantly, it lives beautifully.

Smart Living, Standardised Across Every Unit

Behind the aesthetics is a practical smart-home story. The mirrors support both iOS and Google smart-home ecosystems, with AI-assisted features — so whether a guest lives in the Apple world or the Google world, the residence speaks their language. And because every bathroom from the open-plan units to the four-bedroom duplexes carries the same integrated configuration, operations stay simple: one standard to maintain, one experience to promise, no “which unit type gets the good bathroom” compromises.

For serviced-residence operators, that standardisation is operational gold. Housekeeping, maintenance, and guest support all get simpler when every bathroom works the same way. For residents, it means the technology is simply there — morning news and weather on the mirror, voice control in two major ecosystems, and the quiet confidence that the residence was finished to a single standard.
