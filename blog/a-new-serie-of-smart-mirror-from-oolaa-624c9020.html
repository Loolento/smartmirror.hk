Oolaa Round Smart Mirror with LED Ring Light

May 6, 2023

Circles calm rooms. Oolaa's new smart mirror series takes that oldest of design truths and wires it for the future: a round smart mirror with LED light running the full circumference, made for luxury residential bathrooms. Behind the glass sits the complete hi-tech smart home experience; in front of it, nothing but a beautiful circle of light.

Why round

Bathrooms are full of hard lines — tile grids, rectangular vanities, straight shower glass. A round mirror breaks that geometry the way a good sentence breaks a paragraph: it gives the eye somewhere to rest. Designers have known this forever; it is why the round mirror above a console is a classic in hospitality and high-end residential work.

The circle also flatters the technology inside it. A rectangular smart mirror can read as a screen pretending to be a mirror; a round one reads as a mirror that happens to be smart. That distinction matters in a luxury bathroom, where every object is judged on whether it belongs. The round form belongs; the intelligence is a quiet bonus.

Light where you need it: the full-circumference LED

The LED ring follows the entire circumference of the mirror, which solves the oldest problem in bathroom lighting: shadows. A single overhead light sculpts the face into hollows — flattering to no one, honest to nothing. Light arriving evenly from all sides fills those shadows, giving a true, even view for shaving, skincare, and makeup.

Full-perimeter light also photographs the room beautifully, which matters more than vanity admits: this is the mirror in the listing photos, the one guests remember. And because the light is part of the mirror rather than a fixture bolted beside it, the vanity wall stays clean — one object, doing the work of two.

Smart inside, serene outside

Behind the glass, the round mirror carries the full Oolaa intelligence: the in-house interface designed for the mirror, screen mirroring with “No Login. Just Cast.”, Cantonese-capable voice assistance, and the information residents actually check — time, weather, schedule, health data. The technology never shouts; the surface stays a calm circle of glass and light until the moment information is wanted.

That restraint is deliberate. A luxury bathroom is a place of decompression, and a mirror that behaved like a billboard would fail the room even while passing every spec sheet. Oolaa's software team designs for the mirror as a medium — glanceable, calm, dismissible — so the intelligence serves the serenity instead of competing with it.

Where the round mirror belongs

Over a double vanity in a master bathroom, a pair of round mirrors turns the morning routine into a composed ritual — his and hers, each with its own light and its own information. In a powder room, a single round mirror becomes the statement piece guests actually comment on. In a hotel suite, it photographs well, cleans simply, and gives guests a memorable interaction with the room's technology without a learning curve.

Sizing and mounting reward a short conversation: ceiling height, vanity width, and sightlines all shape the right diameter and hang height. Because Oolaa supplies factory-direct, even unusual vanities are a conversation rather than a compromise — custom diameters can be discussed, not just catalog sizes. As a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen, Oolaa supplies and supports these installations directly — the team advising on placement is the team that built the mirror.
