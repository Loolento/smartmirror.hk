Smart Mirrors in Kerry's Mont Verra Luxury Mansions

February 04, 2025

Discover the epitome of smart home technology at Mont Verra, an extravagant house development in Hong Kong by Kerry Properties, valued at an impressive US$128 million in 2024. This is a project where every detail of the residence is expected to justify the address — and the bathrooms are no exception. We proudly supplied 64 state-of-the-art Oolaa Smart Mirrors for the master bathrooms of this exquisite residence.

The master bathroom, finished properly

In a home at this level, the master bathroom is not a utility room. It is where the day begins and ends, and its mirror is the most-used surface in it. Fitting that surface with a smart mirror — one that presents time, weather and the day's information on the glass, and settles back into a clean reflection when it is not needed — is how a luxury bathroom answers a modern morning.

Sixty-four mirrors went into Mont Verra's master bathrooms. That consistency matters: a development of this calibre doesn't experiment on its buyers. It specifies a product that has already proven itself, and fits it as standard.

Consider what a master bathroom mirror witnesses in a single morning: the weather checked before choosing the day's clothes, the schedule reviewed mid-routine, the time kept in view without a phone on the counter. A smart mirror folds all of that into the one surface that is already being looked at. In a residence like Mont Verra, where the master suite is a private retreat rather than a passageway, that quiet usefulness is the luxury — technology that serves the routine without once asking to be admired.

Built for bathrooms, not adapted to them

A bathroom is a hostile place for ordinary electronics: steam, splashes, humidity, daily use. Oolaa's bathroom mirrors are engineered for exactly that environment, with IP65 protection, a built-in defogger that keeps the glass clear after a hot shower, and motion detection that wakes the display as you approach. It is the difference between a gadget placed in a bathroom and a product born for one — and it is why Oolaa is Hong Kong's market leader in bathroom TV and smart bathroom mirrors.

Why Mont Verra chose Oolaa

Behind the mirrors stands Oolaa (Viewdex Limited): a Hong Kong company, factory-direct from its own plant in Shenzhen, with more than 31,000 installations across Hong Kong and Macau and over 20 years in business. For a developer like Kerry Properties, that profile carries weight — a local partner, its own factory, and a track record measured in tens of thousands of bathrooms.

Embrace the future of living: at Mont Verra, it is already fitted, one mirror in every master bathroom.

There is also a discipline behind the number. Sixty-four mirrors of one model, fitted to one standard, across one project: that is what specification looks like when a developer is protecting a reputation. Every master bathroom in the development promises the same experience, and every one of those promises is backed by the same manufacturer, the same factory, and the same installed base across Hong Kong and Macau.

There is a service story behind the specification as well. A developer fitting 64 mirrors is not buying 64 objects; it is buying the certainty that every one of them can be supported, serviced, and — if a resident ever needs it — replaced by a manufacturer who is still there. A Hong Kong company with its own factory and a 20-year installed base is, for a project of this value, the conservative choice. Mont Verra's bathrooms are what that conservatism looks like when it is done well.
