Oolaa Innovation: Cantonese Voice Smart Mirror

May 23, 2020

Oolaa — then operating under the name JSA — started developing the smart mirror in 2017 with in-house software engineering and continuous technical development. Years later, that decision still defines the company: real R&D, real factory, and voice control that speaks Hong Kong's language.

In-house engineering since 2017

Many smart mirror brands are assemblers: they buy a display, buy a mirror, add commodity software and sell the bundle. Oolaa took the harder road in 2017, building its smart mirror platform on in-house software engineering and committing to continuous technical development year after year.

In-house engineering changes what a product can become. When the software team sits inside the company, features are not limited to what a third-party SDK allows — they follow what users actually ask for. Bug fixes do not wait on a vendor's release cycle. And when a developer or hotel chain needs a customization, the conversation happens with the people who wrote the code. That depth is invisible in a product photo and decisive in a ten-year building lifecycle. It is also why Oolaa can keep improving products that are already installed — software updates flow from the same team that built the mirror, long after the delivery truck has left.

Cantonese voice command: mature and exclusive

The flagship result of that engineering investment is Oolaa's mature and exclusive Cantonese voice command function. Users can schedule and organize their daily life with seamless mother-language control — speaking to the mirror the way they speak to family.

This matters more than it sounds. Voice interfaces live or die on naturalness: a system that understands Cantonese — its tones, its particles, its everyday phrasing — gets used daily, while a system that forces English or Mandarin becomes a party trick. Oolaa's Cantonese capability was not a localization afterthought; it was developed as a core competence, refined over years of real-world use in Hong Kong homes. Combined with Google Assistant support, it gives households both a deeply local voice experience and a global platform underneath.

Factory-direct: our own Shenzhen factory

Software is only half the story. Oolaa (Viewdex Limited) is a Hong Kong company with its own factory in Shenzhen — factory-direct, not a trading company. Hardware and software evolve together under one roof, which is why the mirrors can carry metal back covers, custom glass constructions and commercial-grade build quality instead of consumer-gadget compromises.

For project buyers, factory-direct means accountability: one company designs it, builds it, installs it and supports it. There is no chain of resellers to navigate when something needs attention five years after handover — a practical consideration that developers and hotel operators weigh heavily, and one that trading companies simply cannot match.

The record: 20+ years, 31,000+ installations

Innovation claims are cheap; installed bases are not. Oolaa brings 20+ years in business and 31,000+ installations across Hong Kong and Macau — and market leadership in Hong Kong bathroom TV and kitchen TV, categories the company essentially defined locally.

That scale feeds back into innovation: tens of thousands of real installations generate the usage data, the edge cases and the feature requests that shape the next generation. A startup can promise; an installed base proves. Across eight product lines — Smart Mirror, Bath/Mirror TV, Kitchen/Smart TV, Outdoor TV, Ai-Play Mirror, Axis, Smart Bath Panel and Ai School Kiosk — Oolaa keeps doing what it started in 2017: engineering the product itself, in-house, and making it speak the user's language.
